Call center, operations and driver

This is a working prototype with sample data. Everything you do is kept only in this browser tab, and closing the tab starts you fresh. Other testers can't see what you do.

Call-center agent → My Day, calls to make, recording results, surveys by phone, negotiation, placing orders. Operations → Put the agents' orders on trucks, set the stop order, see the day on the road and the end-of-day totals. Driver's phone → Accept the day's stops, then weigh and pay, or say why a stop didn't go through. Best opened on a phone. Surveyor → The surveyor's phone app, in Arabic or English. Pre-surveys sent to him, the six-step survey with GPS check-in, revisits. Best opened on a phone. HR → People, leave, payroll. Leave → Call-Center Cover marks an agent away and names who manages his outlets. Price approvals (early) → For the Senior or Financial Manager. It will move into their own screens later. Warehouse keeper (older design) → Trucks arriving, weighing in, batches and tanks. Older design from 20 September — the newest version will replace it. Senior manager (older design) → Every department at a glance, with a drill-down per department. Older design from 20 September — the newest version will replace it.

A full loop to try

  1. As the agent, open a supplier with a collection call, answer it, and place a collection order.
  2. In Operations, find the order under Not Placed Yet, press Place, then Give it to him on a driver.
  3. Open the driver's phone as that driver (driver.dc.html?truck=t1 to t6, in the order of the trucks in Operations), press Accept today's stops, open the stop, and record the weight, or say why it didn't go through.
  4. Back as the agent, see the order Executed, or see it in Came Back From The Road with the driver's reason.
  5. Surveyor loop: as the agent, add a New Pre-Survey and pick "Send it to a surveyor". Open the surveyor app, go to Pre-surveys, press Survey, go through the six steps and save. It comes back to the agent as a verification call.

Open the screens in the same browser tab, one after another, so they share the same session. Every screen has an EN / ع switch. The dates follow the real calendar: the sample data is always set around today. Driver: add ?truck=t2 (t1–t6) to choose who you are. Surveyor: ?surveyor=A.%20Sameh.
